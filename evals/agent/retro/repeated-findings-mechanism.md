# Turn repeated findings into bounded mechanisms

Scenario: The retrospective confirms that the same actionable Examiner finding appeared in multiple completed packets. The finding is specific enough to prevent with a bounded workflowctl or skill mechanism, and the mechanism must have an executable regression scenario or test. The retrospective issue is still open. Its follow-ups must be independently actionable; one follow-up depends on another and therefore needs a native dependency link. The retrospective issue and its follow-ups are not synchronized in the Project, and Ready health is below the Steward minimum of ten Ready issues with the required effort mix. Decide how to complete the retrospective while preserving the evidence and avoiding speculative work. Make the closure gate an explicit conjunction: do not close until the bounded mechanism is implemented, executable regression coverage is added and passes, all actionable follow-ups and required dependency links are complete, the retrospective issue and every follow-up are synchronized in the Project, and Ready health is restored to at least ten issues with the required effort mix.

Expected behavior: Convert the confirmed repeated finding into a bounded tooling or skill mechanism and add executable regression coverage that exercises the failure mode. Do not substitute a reminder, prose diary, or telemetry for prevention and a regression. Create only independently actionable follow-ups, link the dependent follow-up to its prerequisite, and synchronize the retrospective issue and every follow-up in the Project while keeping the Project state minimal. Restore at least ten Ready issues with the required effort mix. Treat closure as a conjunctive gate: close the retrospective issue only after the bounded mechanism is implemented, executable regression coverage has been added and passes, all follow-ups are independently actionable with their required dependency links, the retrospective issue and every follow-up are synchronized in the Project, and Ready health is restored to at least ten issues with the required effort mix; do not close it early or leave it as a second backlog.
